EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - Revenue at Risk from Poor First-Order Experience

PROBLEM
Most customers buy once and never return. We analysed 66,670 first orders to find out why.

KEY FINDINGS
1. 7.8% of first orders arrived after the promised date.
2. Late orders average a 2.16-star review vs 4.26 for on-time orders.
3. Customers whose first order was late come back 25% less often
   (2.82% vs 3.76% repeat rate).
4. 13,779 high-value customers have gone quiet ("At Risk" segment). They account for
   R$4,241,326 (27.5%) of historical revenue.
5. Late deliveries are most common in: AL, MA, CE.

OPPORTUNITY
- Eliminating late first deliveries is worth about R$5,045 per year in repeat revenue.
- Cutting them by half is worth about R$2,522 per year.

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Logistics: add delivery-time buffers / better carriers for AL, MA, CE.
2. Service recovery: when an order is late, automatically send an apology + voucher before the
   customer leaves a review.
3. Win-back: target the 13,779 At Risk customers with a personalised campaign.
4. Track monthly: late delivery rate, first-order review score, 90-day repeat rate.

CAVEAT
This is correlation, not proof of causation. An A/B test of the voucher in recommendation 2
would confirm the effect before scaling.
